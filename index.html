Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Teen Quiz Malaysia ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the App. The App can be used anonymously.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: We generate a unique device identifier to track quiz scores and progress locally on your device.
  • Quiz Performance Data: We collect quiz results, scores, and completion statistics to provide you with personalized learning insights.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you interact with the App, including questions attempted, topics studied, and time spent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • Track your quiz performance and progress
  • Generate personalized statistics and insights
  • Improve the App's content and user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance educational content

3. Data Storage

Quiz results and preferences are stored locally on your device and on our secure servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for:

  • Cloud Storage: Cloudinary for image hosting
  • AI Services: Google Gemini AI for generating educational explanations

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for educational purposes and may be used by students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Data Retention

We retain your quiz data as long as you use the App. You can clear your local data at any time by uninstalling the App.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored on our servers
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of data collection (though this may limit App functionality)

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date.
